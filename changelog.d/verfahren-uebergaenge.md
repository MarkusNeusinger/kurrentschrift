### Added

- **The Übergänge route has a process page, and the register gate now demands
  its ledger lines.** Twenty §14 entries between `aug29` and `sep08` measured
  the join grammar — J1 to J6, the two sensors S1 and S2, the `aug30` basket
  round — with no `verfahren-*.md` to land on, which is why rounds 5, 6 and 7
  each shipped without one; the round 7 filing named that as a finding. Author
  decision A43 of 2026-09-09 creates
  `docs/reference/verfahren-uebergaenge.md` in the shape of its four siblings:
  Steckbrief, current state, one ledger row per measured arm, every number a
  dated quote from the entry it belongs to. `tools.docs_register` learned the
  route with it, so an Übergänge entry without its dated ledger row now fails
  the „Docs-Register" job the same way a Kette entry does.
- **The page is honest about not being a duel route.** Kette, Lotse, InkSight
  and Nullprobe compete to follow the ink of a written word; Übergänge is the
  layer underneath that all four compose on, and it earns a ledger because its
  arms move adopted defaults — today the exit trim (A37), tomorrow whatever
  replaces it. `verfahren.md` says so under its route table and also says why
  Laufform, Lineal and Feder, which appear in the same register column,
  deliberately have no page.

  section did not exist. Expanding it over every entry in `ROUTE_PAGES` would
  have demanded a ledger row for a measurement that never happened, so the
  duel four are now their own named tuple.

  Kringel work.** The R4 rescue row in `tintenfolger.md` §7.9 had one path

  The row records it where it binds; the five rescue paths beside it are
