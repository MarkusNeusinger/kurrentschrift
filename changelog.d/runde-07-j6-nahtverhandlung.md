### Added

- **The blind word round on the J6 seam negotiation is judged, filed and booked
  as an honest negative.** Round 7 — 75 screens, the shipped production (A37,
  exit trim on) against the same stack plus seam negotiation, placement pinned
  — goes 14 : 9 to the BASE among the 23 decided screens (39.1 % candidate
  against a ≥ 60 % threshold) at 63.5 % "no difference" against a ≤ 25 %
  threshold. Reported per class as the pre-registration requires and never
  pooled: `naht-stark` 9 : 10 at 34.5 % ties, `naht-schwach` 0 : 4 at 85.7 %,
  and the six null controls answered correctly six times out of six. The
  instrument is clean where it can be checked — twelve mirrored repeats, ten
  naming the same ARM, and the seven side agreements ARE the seven double
  ties, so no pair ever named the same side while naming one; both
  disagreements are decision-against-tie, never base against candidate. The
  measured reason is dose, not direction: the arm moves the drawing by a median
  of 0.0221 x-heights against the 0.1186 floor of round 5's strong class, and
  within this round the decided screens carry 0.0311 of displacement against
  0.0146 for the tied ones. The author's note, verbatim, says the same thing:
  "nicht besser nur bischen weniger wellen mal da oder dort".
- **The round's own artefacts are in the repo** (`data/humanbench/runde-07-*`):
  the result text, the narrow key with its suspicion class per screen, the
  analysis JSON and a provenance stamp carrying both arm checksums, the root
  export the round was built against and the dirty-worktree flag of its build
  commit. The full key, the payload, the two arm files and the strata file with
  its per-word displacements stay out — the judgement is the one part of the
  chain that cannot be recomputed, so it is the part that is kept.

### Changed

- **J6 is closed, and so is the narrower arm J6b.** The pre-registration was
  deliberately two-stage: a result of ≥ 60 % would have licensed J6b rather
  than the default, and below 60 % the arm is shut. At 39.1 % neither opens, so
  `seam_negotiation` stays off and J6 has now failed twice against two
  different instances — at the ruler on 2026-09-06 (gate (c), the Gleichzug
  doublings of the pair drills rising 3 → 5 on `dp`/`ds`) and at the eye today.
  The two failures say different things and together point a successor
  somewhere: the dose is too large on the `dp`/`ds` two-letter drills and too
  small everywhere else to be seen. Five rescue paths ship with the entry, the sensor
  population first, in `messjournal.md` §14 and `tintenfolger.md` §7.9/§7.11.
- **The word mode now records that it has a visibility floor.**
  `menschliche-bewertung.md` §8a carries it as an observation and a BUILD
  obligation, not as a threshold — a threshold read off a round's own class
  declaration is the same re-tuning the pre-registration practice exists to
  prevent. What it does require is that a round which calls a class "visible"
  names in advance the displacement at which it claims that, and justifies it
  against the two measured points (0.0221 xh → 34.5 % ties, 0.1186 xh →
  9.7 %). The alternative it names is a frame rather than a threshold: a round
  that shows the seam itself instead of hiding it inside a whole word.
