### Added

- **The decision that the author's own hand becomes the hand the site
  writes in.** `docs/concepts/vision.md` gains a „Drei Rollen“ section that
  separates the three kinds of writing material the project works with and
  says what each is for: the public-domain plates stay the historical form
  basis — the geometry the canonical templates are fitted to, and the
  Schriftkunde evidence, with the ductus over them the author's own work as
  ever; the written word specimens of the 1922 Sütterlin plate stay ground
  truth for tracing and the frozen ruler the benches measure against; and the
  author's own hand becomes the DELIVERED writing hand of the public pages —
  hero, Federprobe, practice sheet, and the reading quiz the author names,
  where one didactic sub-question stays open — once its Bestand covers the
  alphabet and the transitions. The reasons are recorded with it: a pen,
  ink, paper and capture device recorded as the hand's standing setup with
  each Fassung carrying its session's effective values, rather than a printed
  plate whose nib has to be reconstructed; a sheet at >= 300 DPI against word
  crops that sit at 30–35 px per x-height, which removes the reading floor
  and makes a merged counter less likely without pretending it can reopen ink
  that physically ran together (the counter chain of 2026-09-06/07 measured
  where that loss happens); letters and transitions written on demand when
  one is missing; and a source whose rights lie wholly with the author. Two
  paths are recorded as rejected — the plate staying the delivery hand, and
  the own hand staying training-only — while historical everyday hands are
  deliberately open, in the author's own words that they are written less
  like a textbook. `docs/proposals/eigenhand-erfassung.md` §2 carries the
  operational half: what „covers the alphabet and the transitions“ reads off
  the existing Bestand (>= 3 accepted Fassungen per glyph from the Bestand's
  glyph layer, the transition-space-weighted Erstbeleg-Quote from the
  report), that no tool or step changes, and that the switch itself is a
  separate later owner decision with a declared re-baseline and a public-copy
  change whose locale keys are listed.
