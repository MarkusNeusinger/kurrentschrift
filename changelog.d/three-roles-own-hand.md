### Added

- **The decision that the author's own hand becomes the hand the site
  writes in.** `docs/concepts/vision.md` gains a „Drei Rollen“ section that
  separates the three kinds of writing material the project works with and
  says what each is for: the public-domain plates stay the historical ductus
  prior and the Schriftkunde evidence, the written word specimens of the
  1922 Sütterlin plate stay ground truth for tracing and the frozen ruler
  the benches measure against, and the author's own hand becomes the
  DELIVERED writing hand of the public pages — hero, reading quiz,
  Federprobe, practice sheet — once its Bestand covers the alphabet and the
  transitions. The reasons are recorded with it: one known pen in one
  session, a scan at ≥ 300 DPI where the plate's word crops sit at 30–35 px
  per x-height (the counter chain of 2026-09-06/07 measured that a merged
  tight loop loses its opening already in the ink evidence and cannot be
  recovered from those crops), letters and transitions that can be written
  on demand when one is missing, and a source whose rights lie wholly with
  the author. Whether historical everyday hands follow later is recorded as
  deliberately open, in the author's own words — they are written less like
  a textbook, which makes them harder. `docs/proposals/eigenhand-erfassung.md`
  §2 carries the operational half: what „covers the alphabet and the
  transitions“ reads off the existing Bestandsbericht (≥ 3 accepted Fassungen
  per glyph, the transition-space-weighted Erstbeleg-Quote), that no tool or
  step changes, and that the switch itself is a separate later owner decision
  with a declared re-baseline. The consequences are listed where the decision
  lives: the locale keys whose captions today name the plate as what writes,
  the provenance notes that stay true and only need an addition, the
  „Legibility over period“ Leitsatz unchanged, and the one sub-question the
  owner's words do not settle — whether the reading quiz keeps the 1922 forms
  as its reading target (open, conservative default: it does).
