### Changed

- **The Flecken eraser gets a 1.5 mm brush.** The first real sheet (B0001)
  carried a smear beside strip S0181 — several toner particles fused into one
  blot 2.3 mm across — which the automatic pass leaves to the brush by design
  (a ceiling that caught it would sit inside the i-dot's size class). With
  0.3 / 0.6 / 1.0 mm the author had to cover it with a row of small circles;
  the fourth size covers it in one click. The server's ceiling of 3.0 mm is
  unchanged; it caps the circle's size, and where the circle lands stays the
  author's call — the brush never was a guard against painting over ink.
