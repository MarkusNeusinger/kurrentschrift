### Fixed

- **The Fleckenmaske now catches the printer's real particles.** The first
  real sheet (B0001, scanned at 600 dpi) measured the laser's specks at
  0.25–1.0 mm across, and the detector's ceiling of 0.6 mm — set before a
  real scan existed — left the two largest to the brush. The ceilings are
  now 1.2 mm and 0.6 mm², calibrated between the measured particles and the
  hand's own i-dots (1.0 mm² and up with the M nib), which the size rule
  keeps out before clearance and position are even asked. A smear beyond
  that stays the brush's.
