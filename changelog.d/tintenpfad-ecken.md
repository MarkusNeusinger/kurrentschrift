### Added

- **Four more measured arms of the Tintenpfad, for the corners at reversal
  points and three named residues, all off by default.** The author saw
  „die ein oder andere Ecke, die komisch aussieht bei den Umkehrpunkten".
  The corner is the thinning's: a hairpin turned where the skeleton ends,
  half a nib short of the ink's tip. `hairpin_tip` (Haken-Spitze) reads the
  tip at every same-strand hairpin as the run ends are read — the strand's
  rest and the ridge walk to the end of the mask, out and back, every
  vertex on ink. `ride_back` (Rückfahrt statt Absetzen) is the ß rule as a
  decoder rule without an ink evidence: a seed lift whose landing lies
  behind the pen on its own strand is ridden back over that rail, so the ß
  stem is written down and up as the hand writes it; the three `muß` rows
  halve their distance to the hand trace. `tip_grey_stop` (Grauwert-Stopp)
  lets every tip walk — run ends and hairpins — stop one step before the
  crop's grey reads paper, the reversal sensor's own test, so a walk no
  longer runs through the pale halo of a round cap. `self_jump`
  (Selbstsprung) lets the decoder pass a node its strand visits twice, which
  removes the two lifts the finer rail had introduced in `will` and
  `Galoppieren`. A fifth arm, a corner instead of a Hermite bow between
  anti-parallel tangents, was measured on its own branch
  (`tintenpfad-ecken-ecke-statt-bogen`) and is not shipped: it never fires
  on this plate, the sharpest transition cosine is −0.43, so the corners
  the author saw were cut-off hairpins, not bridges. Every default run
  stays stroke-identical
  to the delivered Tintenpfad; the numbers of the round stay on the progress
  page and in the PR until a §14 entry books them.
